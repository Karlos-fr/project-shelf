Lactea

  • Android TV apps
  • Stable
  • Kotlin
  • Android TV / Google TV
  • C++ (JNI)

MilkDrop-style music visuals for Android TV, reacting to any music app.

Lactea turns the music played on your TV into psychedelic visuals, like Winamp's famous MilkDrop plugin. It listens to whatever the TV plays (Spotify, Deezer, YouTube Music, Plex…) and plays the original MilkDrop presets through projectM: almost 10,000 visualizations made by the community over twenty years. It is designed for the remote, runs up to 4K, and knows how heavy each preset is, so modest TVs stay smooth.

About the project

MilkDrop made music visible on PCs twenty years ago. Lactea brings that experience to the living-room TV, with the music apps people use today. Testing the whole preset collection on a TV also led to fixes in projectM itself, proposed back to its authors.

Key features

  • Reacts to any music app, even through an HDMI soundbar
  • 9,857 presets, all of them load (projectM fixed for MilkDrop compatibility)
  • Track title and album cover, also inside some visuals
  • Opens by itself when music starts
  • Remote-first: every key can be remapped
  • Up to 4K, smart transitions, slow presets skipped
  • Preset packs downloaded from GitHub, list updated automatically
  • Built-in benchmark of every preset on your TV
  • Six languages: EN, FR, DE, IT, ES, SV

Current limitations

  • Track title and auto-launch need permissions granted once from a computer (adb).
  • Sound sent to Bluetooth devices may not be picked up.
  • Tested on one TV only (Sony BRAVIA, Google TV).

Technologies

Kotlin · Android TV / Google TV · C++ (JNI) · projectM 4.1.8 (fork) · OpenGL ES 3 · Android NDK · CMake · Python
